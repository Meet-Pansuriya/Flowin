# Flowin design guide

Last updated: 2026-09-25

## North star

Use [the approved HTML demo](../design/flowin-demo.html) as the current visual north star, [the Stitch brief](../design/stitch-brief.md) for the original design intent, and [the user's reference screenshot](../design/reference-editorial-site.png) for the editorial visual language. These are guides, not sources of product claims. The screenshot belongs to another brand: borrow its composition and feel, not its logos, words, or business imagery.

## Look and feel

- Premium, editorial, industrial, confident; warm rather than clinical.
- Warm off-white `#f8f7f4` background, charcoal `#20211f` text and dark section, fine gray `#d8d8d2` dividers, restrained burnt orange `#c64a13` highlight. Tune the orange against Flowin's real black/red logo when production assets are selected.
- Large Manrope-style headings with tight letter spacing; DM Sans-style body copy. The demo currently uses these fonts and is the practical style reference.
- Generous vertical rhythm, disciplined asymmetric grids, small eyebrow labels, thin outlined cards, simple line illustrations, and one highlighted orange card within the range grid.
- A wide cinematic industrial image, then a later full-width charcoal section with light text. The user approved existing/generated imagery; keep generated scenes identified as illustrative so they are not mistaken for documentary images of Flowin's premises.
- Quiet interactions: clear focus states and restrained hover transitions. No dashboard styling or ornamental animation that slows product discovery.

## Intended page structure

1. Homepage: compact navigation and catalogue action; editorial hero; product-range overview for uPVC plumbing, cPVC plumbing, SWR drainage, and agriculture; application/company story; enquiry and catalogue calls to action.
2. Product overview: clear four-range navigation and scannable family cards.
3. Product-family detail: verified name/photo/description, key sizes or specifications where confirmed, catalogue link, enquiry action.

On mobile, retain the same hierarchy, stack content cleanly, keep the catalogue action accessible, and reflow tables for reading rather than shrinking them.

## Review rule

Before adding a section, ask: does it help a customer understand Flowin's actual products, find a catalogue entry, or contact the company? If not, leave it out. If a design decision conflicts with verified content or readability, choose clarity and document the deviation.
