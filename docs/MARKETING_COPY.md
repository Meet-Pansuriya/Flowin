# Trade-focused copy rewrite

Updated 14 October 2026. Rewritten across all 30 rendered routes, including shared range and product-family templates, navigation, footer, catalogue, order-list tool and 404 page.

## Positioning

One Flowin brand across uPVC, cPVC, SWR and agriculture. Speak to dealers and plumbers across India. Company pages explain the manufacturer behind the range; range and family pages help visitors turn the products they need into a purchase discussion.

The homepage leads with “One brand for plumbing, drainage and agriculture.” About Flowin introduces “The manufacturer behind Flowin” and explains manufacturing, quality control and range breadth. Family introductions identify the particular products and options a buyer can select, rather than describing table transcription. Calls to action use “Explore Flowin products,” “Open catalogue” and “Build your order list.”

## Evidence

PDF page 2 of the current 16-page 2026 catalogue supports establishment in 2013, professionally managed manufacturing, modern manufacturing practices, stringent quality control, focus on quality/durability/consistent performance, four product systems and residential/commercial/agricultural/industrial applications. Quality language expresses that published manufacturing commitment; it adds no test results, guarantees, certification scope or measured performance.

Product options and dimensions use the existing catalogue-backed data. No dealer margins, stock, delivery promise, testimonials or contact details were added. The order-list tool prepares a list for the visitor to send to their dealer; it does not place an order or automatically send an enquiry.

## Verification

The rendered HTML of all 70 catalogue tables matched the pre-change baseline exactly. Cloudflare build, all local URL checks and SEO checks passed. Homepage and About were reviewed at desktop/phone widths; a SWR family page was checked for the new introduction, unchanged data and correct order-list link. Existing imagery and SEO indexing/canonical/sitemap behavior remain in place.

The published company facts establish a clearer message. More specific competitive reasons to choose Flowin still need business evidence: actual dealer support, distribution/service arrangements, product testing and customer experience.
