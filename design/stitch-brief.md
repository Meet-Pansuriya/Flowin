# Flowin website design brief for Stitch

Design a responsive B2B product showcase website for Flowin Pipes & Fittings, an Indian manufacturer of plumbing and drainage pipes and fittings. This is a visual design exploration, not an online store.

Create a coherent set of three web screens:

1. Homepage: an industrial product hero, clear headline about complete piping solutions, prominent **Explore Products** and **Download 2026 Catalogue** actions, four product-range cards, applications, company introduction, and contact call to action.
2. Product catalogue overview: obvious navigation for uPVC plumbing, cPVC plumbing, SWR drainage, and agriculture pipes and fittings. Use scannable product-family cards so visitors can find pipes, elbows, tees, couplers, valves, and related fittings quickly.
3. Product-family detail page for uPVC elbows: large product photo, concise description, relevant standard/specification area, readable size and packing table, link to the full catalogue PDF, and an enquiry call to action.

The website's purpose is to showcase products and provide the catalogue. Do not add a cart, checkout, account, stock level, invented certification claim, fabricated company statistics, or fake testimonial. The PDF is the official price list; product pages focus on specifications and discovery.

Art direction: use the supplied logistics website screenshot as the primary visual reference. Capture its editorial feel, not its branding or content: warm off-white background, very generous vertical spacing, oversized clean sans-serif headings, tiny eyebrow labels, compact understated navigation, fine dividers and line icons, one burnt-orange accent, wide cinematic industrial photography, and a later full-width charcoal section with light text. Use an asymmetric but disciplined grid and restrained hover treatments. Make the site feel premium, confident, and industrial without looking like a SaaS dashboard or a generic plumbing template.

Adapt the reference to Flowin: replace the warehouse/forklift subject with authentic pipes, fittings, installation, manufacturing, or warehouse imagery relevant to Flowin. Replace the reference's industry/service cards with Flowin's real product ranges and applications. A product-range grid can borrow the reference's thin outlined cards and single orange featured card, but should use appropriate pipe/fitting line drawings. Keep Flowin's real black/red logo legible; the orange accent may be tuned to harmonize with it. Do not invent partner logos, customers, awards, or certifications to fill visual space.

Make desktop and mobile versions feel like the same design. On mobile, stack the editorial sections cleanly, keep the navigation and catalogue action easy to reach, and redesign specification tables for legibility rather than shrinking them. Keep the catalogue download prominent.

Use real range names from the 2026 catalogue: uPVC plumbing, cPVC plumbing, SWR drainage, and agriculture. Treat all generated product imagery and copy as design placeholders until checked against the Flowin catalogue and approved assets.
